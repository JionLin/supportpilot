# Shipping & Delivery

## Domestic shipping (United States)

| Method | ETA | Cost |
|--------|-----|------|
| Standard | 5–7 business days | Free over $50, otherwise $5.99 |
| Express | 2–3 business days | $12.99 |
| Overnight | 1 business day | $24.99 |

## Tracking

After your order ships you receive a tracking number by email. Tracking updates usually appear within 24 hours of handoff to the carrier.

## International

We ship to select countries. Duties and taxes are the customer's responsibility. International transit typically takes 7–14 business days.
