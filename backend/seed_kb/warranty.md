# Warranty

Most products include a **12-month limited warranty** against manufacturing defects.

## Covered

- Defects in materials or workmanship under normal use.
- Failure of zippers, seams, or electronics included with the product.

## Not covered

- Normal wear and tear, misuse, accidents, or unauthorized repairs.
- Damage from improper washing or storage.
- Consumable accessories (batteries, filters) after 90 days.

## Claims

Contact support with your order number, photos of the defect, and a short description. Approved claims may be repaired, replaced, or refunded at our discretion.
