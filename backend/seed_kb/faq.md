# Frequently Asked Questions

## How do I track my order?

Provide your order number (format like `A1001`) and the email used at checkout. We look up shipping events from the carrier.

## Can I change my shipping address?

Address changes are possible only before the label is created. After that, contact the carrier with your tracking number.

## Do you offer gift receipts?

Yes. Reply to your confirmation email and ask for a gift receipt; prices are omitted.

## How do I book a call-back?

Ask support for available slots. Choose a time and confirm your name and email. You will receive a booking confirmation.

## When should I talk to a human?

If you are upset, the request is outside policy, tools fail repeatedly, or you simply prefer a person — ask for a human agent and we will open a ticket.
